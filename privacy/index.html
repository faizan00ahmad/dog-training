Privacy policy

How we handle your information, cookies and advertising tools.

Template notice: this policy is a starting point. Have it reviewed against the laws that apply to your business before launch.

What we collect

When you fill in our booking form we receive your name, email, optional phone number, your dog's details and your message. We use it to reply to you and plan your session.

Cookies and advertising tools

With your consent we use Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and the performance of our ads. If you decline, these tools are blocked from storing identifiers on your device. You can change your choice any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. Analytics and advertising providers receive data only when you consent. Our form and scheduling providers process data on our behalf.
