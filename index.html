FAQ

Dog training FAQs for Austin owners

Should the whole family attend dog training sessions?

Ideally the whole household attends, so everyone uses the same cues. If that isn't possible, one person can attend and we'll write a plan to share.

Do you train reactive dogs at home in Austin?

Yes. Reactive dogs are a big part of our work. We keep your dog below threshold and never push them past what they can handle.

How many dog training sessions does my dog need?

Many households see a big change in one 2-hour session, with the plan and support doing the rest. Complex cases may need a follow-up, and we'll tell you honestly after the call.

What if it rains or I need to reschedule?

Outdoor sessions move under cover or indoors where possible. Give 24 hours' notice to reschedule at no charge.

Do you offer a refund if my dog training does not work?

Training depends on practice at home, so we don't promise a fixed outcome. If you're not satisfied after the first month, we'll adjust the plan or offer a prorated refund on unused time.
