Our standards

What you can expect from every session

These aren't marketing lines. They're the rules we train by, and we'll put them in writing.

01

Reward-based methods only

No prong, choke or shock collars, and no physical corrections. Dogs learn faster when they aren't afraid.

02

A written plan you can follow

You leave with step-by-step homework, so progress doesn't depend on remembering what we said.

03

Honest timelines and pricing

We tell you what's realistic for your dog before you commit to a package, including when you don't need one.

Support for the life of your dog

Call or email any time. If a problem comes back, follow-up sessions are included.

FAQ

Dog training questions, answered

Still not sure? Call us on (555) 012-3456 and we'll talk it through.

Book a free call
Should the whole family come to the session?

Ideally, yes. Everyone who handles the dog should use the same cues. If that isn't possible, one person can attend and we'll write a plan to share.

Do you train reactive dogs at home?

Yes. Reactive dogs are a big part of the work. We keep your dog below the point where they react and never push past what they can handle.

How many sessions will my dog need?

Many households see a big change after one two-hour session. Complex cases may need a few follow-ups. We'll tell you honestly after the first visit.

What if it rains or I need to reschedule?

We move outdoor work under cover or indoors where we can. Give us 24 hours' notice to reschedule for free.

Do you offer refunds?

Training depends on practice at home, so we can't promise a fixed result. If you're unhappy after the first month, we'll change the plan or refund unused sessions.
