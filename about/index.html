About Paws & Path, Austin dog trainers

A small team of certified, force-free trainers who work in your home, on your street, with your dog.

Founder

Alex Morgan, CPDT-KA

"Most dogs aren't stubborn. They just haven't been shown what we want in a way they can understand."

Alex started Paws & Path in 2012 after years volunteering with rescue dogs. Since then the team has worked with more than 1,200 dogs across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park and Pflugerville, from nervous rescues to boisterous labs to puppies on their first night home.

Our approach

We train with rewards. Treats, toys, play and praise show your dog what we want, and we manage the environment so they can succeed. We don't use prong, choke or shock collars, and we don't believe in "dominance" or "alpha" theories. Modern behaviour science has moved on, and dogs do better when they aren't afraid.

Why we train at home

Classroom skills often fall apart on your street. By working in your home and on your usual routes, we deal with the real problem in the real place. The whole household can take part, and the plan fits your routine.

What we promise

  • Reward-based methods only.
  • A written plan after every visit.
  • Honest advice about timelines and cost, including when you don't need a package.
  • Phone and email support for the life of your dog.

Credentials and insurance

Alex holds the CPDT-KA credential and keeps up with continuing education. We carry liability insurance and bonding for in-home visits, and follow a published code of ethics. Not sure what to look for in any trainer? Our guide on choosing a dog trainer in Austin lists nine questions to ask.
