Dog obedience training in Austin, TX

Walk without being dragged. Come when you call. Settle when guests arrive. We teach obedience in your home and on your own streets, so it works in real life and not only in a classroom.

From $120 per two-hour sessionReward-based methods onlyIn-home across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville

Who obedience training is for

Most of the families we see aren't chasing show-ring perfection. They want a dog who doesn't pull their arm off, who comes back at the park, and who can be calm when the doorbell rings. If that sounds like you, this is the right place to start.

It suits adult dogs, adolescent dogs (the 8 to 18 month stage can be a handful) and rescue dogs settling into a new home.

What we teach

  • Loose-leash walking on the routes you actually use, whether that's a quiet Mueller street or the Lady Bird Lake trail.
  • Sit, down, stay and wait, including at doors and car doors.
  • Recall. A "come" that survives squirrels and other dogs. See our recall training guide.
  • Settle on a mat so your dog can relax while you eat, work or have visitors.
  • Leave it and drop it for the things your dog shouldn't have.
  • Polite greetings, with no jumping up on people.

How a session works

  1. We talk first. Before we visit we hear about your dog on a free 15-minute call.
  2. We train in your home. About two hours, with everyone in the household welcome.
  3. You get a written plan. Simple steps, short practice sessions, and what to do when things go wrong.
  4. Support afterwards. Phone and email help for the life of your dog.

Everything we do is reward-based. No prong, choke or shock collars. If you want to know why, read our guide to stopping leash pulling.

Pricing

Obedience sessions start at $120 per two-hour visit. Many dogs make clear progress in a single session and keep improving with the practice plan. Packages are available if you'd like ongoing coaching. For a full breakdown of what dog training costs around Austin, see our price guide.

Obedience Training FAQs

How long does dog obedience training take?

Most dogs show clear improvement after one session and keep progressing over two to six weeks of regular practice. Strong habits or high-distraction areas can take longer.

Do you train dogs at my home in Austin?

Yes. We train in your home and on your normal walking routes across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park and Pflugerville.

What age can a dog start obedience training?

Puppies can start learning basics from 8 weeks. Adult and senior dogs can learn too, at any age.

Do you use treats or collars?

We use rewards such as treats, toys and praise. We never use prong, choke or shock collars.
