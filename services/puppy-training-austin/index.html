Private sessions or group classes?

Private in-home sessions are ideal for the practical stuff: house training, nipping and routines. Small group classes (six puppies at most) are great for socialisation once your puppy has had their vaccinations. Many families do one private visit first, then a class. Not sure which is right? Read in-home vs group training.

When to start

As early as 8 weeks. The socialisation window matters, so the earlier we help you plan safe experiences, the better. If your puppy is older than six months, don't worry. Adolescent dogs benefit from our obedience sessions.

Puppy Training FAQs

When should I start training my puppy?

From the day you bring them home, usually around 8 weeks. Early habits are far easier to shape than to change.

Can my puppy go to class before finishing vaccinations?

Check with your vet. Many puppy classes require at least the first vaccinations, and private in-home sessions are a safe way to start earlier.

How long does house training take?

Many puppies are mostly reliable in 4 to 8 weeks with a consistent routine, though full reliability can take a few months.

Do you offer puppy classes in Austin?

Yes, small group classes capped at six puppies. Get in touch for the next dates.
